## 🎓 Lessons & Best Practices

### What Works Well
✅ **Clear distinction** between free and paid
✅ **Non-aggressive** upgrade prompts
✅ **Real value** in free version
✅ **Obvious benefits** in paid version
✅ **Multiple touchpoints** for conversion
✅ **Professional presentation** throughout

### What to Avoid
❌ Don't cripple free version too much
❌ Don't spam upgrade messages
❌ Don't hide upgrade path
❌ Don't make pricing confusing
❌ Don't ignore free users
