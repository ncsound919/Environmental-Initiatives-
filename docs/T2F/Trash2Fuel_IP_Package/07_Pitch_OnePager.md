# One‑Pager (External)
**Trident Platform** turns messy municipal waste into three things communities actually want:
1) **Materials back first** (styrene/rEPS, rPET, aggregates), 
2) **Clean fuel intermediates** (polished plastic oil sold as spec’d feed; toll‑upgrade only as needed), and 
3) **Visible clean miles** for scooters and small fleets backed by a public compliance dashboard.

**Why it wins:** 70–90 day pilots, low‑pressure kit, labor‑forward ops, open MRV, and no on‑vehicle chemistry. Cities get cleaner air and fewer trucks to the landfill; partners get reliable feed; the public gets receipts.
