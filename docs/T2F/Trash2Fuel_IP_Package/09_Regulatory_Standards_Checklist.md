# Regulatory & Standards Checklist
- Air permit class and CEMS requirements; fence-line monitors.
- Waste handling, stormwater, fire code; hazardous liquids storage.
- Solid recovered fuel specs (e.g., EN 15359) for any solids sold.
- Fuel‑intermediate buyer specs (Cl, water, metals, dist curve) documented in SKUs.
- Safety interlocks, hot work, LEL sensors; operator training and PPE.
- Community benefits agreement and EJ engagement plan.
