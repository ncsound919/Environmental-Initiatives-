# Enablement & Embodiments (Engineering Narrative)
## Intake & Routing
- Conveyor + optical/NIR sort + manual QC. Inbound bales tested for moisture, Cl, ash, fines. Non‑conforming loads auto‑routed to non‑fuel products (rEPS sheets, aggregates, foam glass).

## Lane A — PS/EPS to materials-first
- Option A1: Mechanical densify → extrusion into panels/blocks.
- Option A2: Depolymerize under vacuum/catalyst → styrene‑rich cut; condenser train; polishing; closed‑loop solvent recovery.

## Lane B — Polyolefins to polished oil
- Thermal depolymerization → three‑cut vacuum fractionation (light/mid/heavy).
- Adsorber skid with swappable clay/alumina/carbon beds; ΔP and breakthrough color guide changeouts.
- Wash train: caustic/acid/water → coalescer → dry → cartridge filtration.
- Blend to buyer spec; worst cuts sent to toll hydrotreater.

## Lane C — Wet/dry residues
- Wet: HTL or digestion to biocrude/biogas for site heat/power.
- Dry: Gasification → gas cleanup (particulate, tars, HCl, H₂S, alkali) → onsite CHP or synthesis if contracted.

## MRV Layer
- CEMS on stacks; fence-line VOC/LEL/PM; data logger publishes JSON & dashboard; automatic interlocks reduce load on exceedance.

## Micromobility Coupling
- Primary: e‑scooter battery swap cabinets powered by plant or PPA, with kWh and miles attributed from plant output.
- Secondary: small methanol pilot depots with sealed quick‑connect tanks and aldehyde‑oxidation catalysts on vehicles.

## Safety & Ops
- Low‑pressure transfer, bonding/grounding, hose‑in‑hose, spill kits, operator PPE.
- Labor‑first SOPs for adsorber changeouts, sampling, and QA/QC.
