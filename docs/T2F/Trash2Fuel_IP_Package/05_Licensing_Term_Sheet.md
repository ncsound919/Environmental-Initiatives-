# Licensing Term Sheet (Template)
**Structure:** Field‑of‑Use + Territory licenses with tech transfer package and support hours.

- **Upfront:** $150k–$750k depending on scope (single lane vs full Trident).
- **Royalty:** 2.5%–5.0% of net sales on fuel intermediates and materials products; floor minimums after year 2.
- **Milestones:** Pilot commissioning, first commercial offtake, continuous MRV launch.
- **Audit & Reporting:** Quarterly volume and spec reports; right to audit annually.
- **Branding:** Licensee uses “Trident Platform” and “E‑Blend” marks per brand guide; must operate MRV dashboard publicly.
- **Tech Transfer:** PFDs/P&IDs, SOPs, QA/QC methods, spec sheets, dashboard code, and sample contracts.
- **Exclusions:** No on‑vehicle thermal conversion rights granted.
