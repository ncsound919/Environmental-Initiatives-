# Patent Claims Scaffold (Draft)
## Independent Claim 1 — Process orchestration
A method for valorizing mixed solid waste comprising:
(a) receiving an incoming waste stream and measuring at least chlorine, moisture, ash, and fines; 
(b) routing polystyrene-containing fractions to a materials‑first lane that performs (i) mechanical densification or (ii) depolymerization to styrene; 
(c) routing polyolefin‑rich fractions to a finishing lane comprising distillative fractionation, adsorption‑based halogen and metals removal, aqueous/acid washing, and controlled blending to produce a saleable intermediate fuel or cracker feed, wherein hydrotreating is performed only via third‑party facilities as needed; 
(d) routing wet organics to hydrothermal liquefaction or anaerobic digestion, and dry non‑recyclables to gasification with gas cleanup; and 
(e) publishing monitored emissions and mass‑balance data to a public interface with automatic process derating or shutdown upon exceedance.

## Independent Claim 2 — Compliance monitoring & governance
A system comprising continuous emissions monitors, fence‑line sensors, and a data layer that computes product carbon intensity and criteria pollutants in real time, renders a public dashboard, and enforces automated interlocks that constrain production when thresholds are exceeded.

## Independent Claim 3 — Micromobility offtake coupling
A method that allocates plant‑level energy outputs to micromobility fleets by cryptographically accounting for waste processed, energy produced, and vehicle‑miles delivered, with field‑of‑use licensing for urban delivery fleets.

## Dependent Claims (examples)
- The method of claim 1, wherein EPS is pre‑dissolved in a recyclable solvent to reduce transport volume prior to depolymerization.
- The method of claim 1, wherein adsorption uses alumina/clay/carbon in swappable cartridges with operator‑controlled replacement.
- The method of claim 1, further comprising specification windows conforming to EN 15359 for solid recovered fuel offered to industrial customers.
- The system of claim 2, wherein public data includes rolling 24‑hour averages and raw minute‑level logs.
- The system of claim 3, wherein allocation to fleets prioritizes zero‑tailpipe platforms (battery swap scooters) and secondarily methanol‑calibrated small engines with aldehyde catalysts.
