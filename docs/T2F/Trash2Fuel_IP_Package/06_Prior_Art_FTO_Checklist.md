# Prior Art & FTO Checklist
- **Search domains:** plastic pyrolysis polishing trains; EPS depolymerization to styrene; MRV dashboards for waste/fuel plants; EN 15359 SRF integration; hydrotreater‑optional finishing lines; micromobility energy attribution systems.
- **Red flags to check:** patents on adsorption‑wash sequences specific to plastic oils; solvent‑based EPS densification before depoly; public emissions dashboards linked to interlocks; energy‑to‑miles accounting claims.
- **Mitigations:** claim orchestration logic, routing thresholds, MRV governance, and licensing framework rather than commodity unit ops alone.
