# Trash→Value Trident Platform — Invention Summary
**Date:** 2025-09-10
**Working Title:** Trident Platform for Waste-Stream Triage, Materials Recovery, and Fuel-Grade Intermediates  
**Inventor:** [Your Name]  
**Field:** Waste valorization, materials recovery, thermochemical conversion, compliance monitoring, micromobility energy provisioning

## Problem
Mixed municipal waste is chaotic. Forcing it into a single “trash→fuel” path creates poor-quality outputs, emissions risk, and capex bloat (hydrotreater/H₂). Cities and fleets want cleaner air and low-volatility energy, not science fair projects.

## Solution (High level)
A **three‑lane modular platform** that:
1) Routes **polystyrene/expanded polystyrene (PS/EPS)** to **monomer-first depolymerization or mechanical reuse** (“materials first”),
2) Routes **polyolefin‑rich residues (PE/PP)** to **polished pyrolysis oil** via **fractionation + adsorption + wash** with **toll hydrotreating only as needed**, and
3) Routes **wet organics** to **hydrothermal/digestion** and **dry non-recyclables** to **clean syngas** for **power/heat or methanol/FT** where viable.

A **spec discipline layer** at intake (moisture/Cl/ash) plus a **public MRV dashboard** (CEMS + fence-line) provides measurable environmental compliance. A **flexible midstream** (low‑pressure liquid transfer, drum/tote logistics) enables distributed micro-plants without owning every upgrader. Optional tie‑in: **micromobility energy** (battery swap cabinets, methanol pilot depots) as demand sinks tied to local waste credits.

## Core Differentiators
- **Waste‑hierarchy enforcement** baked into control logic (materials recovery first, energy for residues).
- **Hydrotreater‑optional finishing** that reaches stable buyer specs with low‑pressure kit (fractionation + adsorption + wash + blending) and **tolling** for the rest.
- **Public MRV compliance layer** (continuous emissions monitoring + open LCA model + automatic shutdown policy) to defuse regulator and EJ objections.
- **Spec’d solid “capsules” (RDF/SRF)** as a **logistics form**, not an on‑vehicle fuel.
- **Micromobility tie‑in**: waste‑to‑kWh/miles accounting for e‑scooter fleets as a visible, low‑emissions offtake.

## Performance Targets (initial)
- EPS/PS: ≥70 wt% styrene-range cut or monomer purity ≥90%, solvent recycle ≥95%.
- Polyolefin oil: Cl ≤ 50 ppm; water ≤ 0.1 wt%; stable distillation curve; metals per buyer spec.
- Syngas (if used): tar ≤ 50 mg/Nm³; HCl/H₂S below catalyst tolerance.
- CEMS: PM, NOx, VOCs within permitted limits with live public dashboard.
